# Editing the Westcott website: staff guide

You can update the website yourself, from any computer, with no coding. Changes appear on the live site about **one to two minutes** after you save.

## Signing in

1. Go to **https://westcottcc.org/admin/** (during review: the preview address + `/admin/`).
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and use your GitHub account. If you don't have access yet, ask the site admin to invite you.

You'll see a list on the left: **Events, Programs, Pages, Settings**.

## Common tasks

### Add an event
1. Click **Events → New Event**.
2. Fill in the name, date, time, location and a one- or two-sentence description. Add a flyer under **Image or flyer** if you have one.
3. Under **Describe the image**, write what the image shows (for example "Flyer for the Fall Health Fair"). This helps visitors who use screen readers.
4. Click **Save**. The event shows on the home page and the Events page until its date passes, then moves to Past events by itself.

### Update a program (new phone number, new schedule, new flyer)
1. Click **Programs**, then the program.
2. Change the field. **When it happens** is the schedule line shown on cards and the Events page.
3. To replace a flyer: under **Links and files**, click the file and upload the new one. Give it a clear name, like `pantry-flyer-2027-jan-jun`.
4. Click **Save**.

### Post this month's senior activity calendar
**Programs → Senior Services → Links and files → "This month's activity calendar"**, upload the new image, Save.

### Add a job opening
**Pages → Jobs → Links**, click **Add**, type the job title as the link text and paste the job posting address. Remove it when the job is filled. Save.

### Show an announcement on every page (snow closing, holiday hours)
**Settings → Contact info, hours, stats and sponsors → Announcement banner.** Turn on **Show the banner**, write the message, Save. Turn it off again when it's over.

### Change hours, phone, address, impact numbers, sponsors or annual reports
All of these are under **Settings → Contact info, hours, stats and sponsors**.

### Update the board or leadership
**Settings → Leadership and Board.** Drag names to reorder.

### Hide something without deleting it
Turn on **Hide from the website (draft)** and Save. Turn it off to bring it back.

## Writing tips

- **Lead with what people need:** what it is, who it's for, when, where, who to call.
- **Short sentences, plain words.** Many visitors read on a phone, and some read English as a second language.
- **Write dates and times in full:** "Thursdays, 5:00 PM to 6:30 PM".
- **Images:** photos under about 2 MB. Flyers as an image (PNG or JPG) *and* include the key details in the text, because text in an image can't be read by screen readers or search engines.
- **Links:** use words that say where the link goes ("Volunteer interest form"), not "click here".

## If something goes wrong

- **The change isn't showing:** wait two minutes and refresh. Still not there? The build may have failed; contact the site admin.
- **You made a mistake:** every saved version is kept. The site admin can restore any earlier version from GitHub.
- **You can't sign in:** ask the site admin to check that your GitHub account has access.

Site admin contacts are listed in [HANDOVER.md](HANDOVER.md).

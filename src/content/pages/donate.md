Westcott Community Center is a nonprofit that provides human service programs and enriching arts and cultural programs that foster stability, growth and well-being in our community. Your gifts let us keep serving our neighbors and the families who rely on us.

You can sponsor a specific program or event, or give to our general operating fund. We are also raising funds for an endowment, so the center can serve more people and meet even more community needs.

## Give by mail
Make your check payable to **Westcott Community Center** and mail it to:

Westcott Community Center<br>821 Euclid Avenue<br>Syracuse, NY 13210

Donations are tax deductible (check with your tax advisor). Thank you for helping improve the lives of our youth, adults and seniors.

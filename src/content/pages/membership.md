title: Membership
description: Become a member to support free and low-cost programs, and get a vote at our annual meeting.
---
Becoming a member helps us keep offering free and low-cost programs to people in need. Members also get:

- a monthly e-newsletter with advance notice of programs, services and special events
- a vote at our annual meeting

An annual family membership is **$55**.

To join, pay the $55 fee through our [donation page](/donate/), or send a check or money order to 821 Euclid Ave, Syracuse, NY 13210.

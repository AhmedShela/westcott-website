The Volunteer Lawyers Project runs free, weekly walk-in clinics at community centers, churches and schools. You can talk to a lawyer in a relaxed setting, often after regular business hours. Volunteer attorneys give free legal advice, information and referrals on any civil legal issue.

This is a walk-in service, so appointments are not available.

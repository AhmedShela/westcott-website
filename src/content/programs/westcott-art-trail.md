Celebrate creativity, community and connection at the Westcott Art Trail, a one-of-a-kind neighborhood experience that brings local art to life on the streets of Westcott. The 25th annual Art Trail took place on Saturday, June 20, 2026.

Neighbors and artists started the Art Trail to show off the talent in our own community. Rooted in Westcott's spirit of diversity, inclusion and creativity, it welcomes everyone to explore, connect and be inspired.

Stroll or drive through the neighborhood as homes, porches, yards and community spaces become pop-up galleries. Meet local painters, photographers, sculptors, printmakers, ceramicists, glass artists, mixed-media makers and more, many showing how they work and selling their art. Live music plays throughout the neighborhood all day.

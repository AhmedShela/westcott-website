Join us every Wednesday from 2:00 to 6:00 PM, June through October (or as long as the growing season allows), in our parking lot along Euclid Avenue.

Since 2007, the Syracuse Eastside Farmers Market has brought fresh produce to the neighborhood in an accessible, family-friendly way that enriches the culture and community life of Syracuse's east side.

## Vendors
Farmers and local pre-packaged food vendors are invited to apply. The Market Manager will contact you within 2 weeks of receiving your application. Sending an application does not guarantee a spot: this is a small to medium market, so we limit duplicate products.

The market is sponsored by Westcott Community Center in partnership with the Farmers' Market Federation of New York.

The New York State Energy Research and Development Authority (NYSERDA) created Regional Clean Energy Hubs to help New Yorkers find and use clean energy programs and opportunities. EnergySmart CNY serves **Cayuga, Cortland, Madison, Onondaga and Oswego** counties.

EnergySmart CNY is a team made up of Westcott Community Center, CNYRPDB and AGREE. Together we grow the clean energy workforce by connecting people with employers, training programs and other resources.

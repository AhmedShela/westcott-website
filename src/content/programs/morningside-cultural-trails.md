**A walking trail network that brings the community together around its art and history, architecture and culture, science and nature, and recreation and fitness.**

The Morningside Cultural Trails network includes more than 10 miles of walking trails on Syracuse's east side, each with its own character. It currently includes the Morningside Cultural Trail, the Morningside Heights Trail, the Oakwood Cemetery Tree Trail, the Morningside Landscape Trail and the Public Arts Pathway. The trails cross three neighborhoods and highlight their cultural, historic, literary, artistic, scientific and recreational treasures.

The [Syracuse Community Geography Program](https://communitygeography.org/) at Syracuse University works with Paul Harvey, a local urban trail enthusiast and Creative Communities Facilitator, to make the maps. Large trail maps are posted on kiosks at Barry Park, Oakwood Cemetery, Westcott Community Center and Ed Smith Pre-K-8 School.

![A Morningside trail](/uploads/morningside-trail-1.webp)

## Who keeps the trails
The trails are maintained by a coalition of the Ed Smith PTO, SUNY ESF, Syracuse University and Oakwood Cemetery (with the Historic Oakwood Cemetery Preservation Association). Many others have joined in, including Westcott Community Center, Boy Scout Troop 1, the ESF student activities group, the Everson Museum, the Onondaga Historical Association, the Barry Park Association, and the Nottingham High School Key Club and Honor Society.

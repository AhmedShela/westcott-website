The Bridge to Manufacturing program prepares people to enter Onondaga Community College's Electromechanical Program and other manufacturing training pathways. It helps you gain the skills and training for high-demand manufacturing careers, with support along the way toward new skills, education and a new career.

Scan the QR code on the flyer, or contact Gail Tosh to learn more.

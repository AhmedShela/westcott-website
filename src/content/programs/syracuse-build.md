Syracuse Build is the regional workforce strategy that supports construction demand on public and private projects. CenterState CEO's construction industry partnerships bring together public agencies, anchor institutions, contractors, unions, training providers and community organizations, so that major projects hire from within the region and growth benefits the community.

Westcott Community Center is proud to work with CenterState CEO and its partners so that construction opportunities with **Micron** and the **I-81 project** have a strong local workforce.

To learn more, attend an information session, or contact our Syracuse Build Coordinator.

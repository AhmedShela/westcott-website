The Syracuse Community Center Collaborative is a partnership of four community centers joined by a memorandum of understanding. Serving the four quadrants of the city, the Collaborative is made up of Westcott Community Center, Syracuse Northeast Community Center, Syracuse Community Connections and Huntington Family Centers.

## Mission
We strengthen Syracuse neighborhoods by supporting families. Our shared human service work helps neighbors reach opportunities to build the lives they want to live.

## What we do
Together we serve 23,000 of Syracuse's most vulnerable residents, while keeping the neighborhood connection and trust built through decades of service. Sharing staff and services lets us fill gaps, meet needs and serve the community more efficiently.

**Aging with health, hope and dignity.** Whether you want friendship, exercise, classes or something fun to do, Westcott Community Center offers it for residents of the City of Syracuse. Stop by to see the center, and tell us what would make your experience better.

## Daily breakfast
Coffee and a continental breakfast, Monday to Thursday, 9:30 to 11:00 AM.

## Daily lunch
A hot, balanced meal for adults 60+, Monday to Thursday. Lunch is served promptly at **noon**. Come early for games, crafts, music and conversation. The suggested contribution is $3.50 (no one is turned away), and guest meals are $7.00. The menu is in our monthly senior newsletter, at the center, and on the PEACE, Inc. website.

## Activities
Before and after lunch, Monday to Thursday: Bingo, Jeopardy, Price is Right, art workshops, seminars, health screenings, wellness support, exercise, movies, tea time and more. See the monthly activity calendar.

## Exercise classes
Free for everyone. **Yoga** on Mondays at 1:00 PM with Cassie, and **Strength & Balance** on Tuesdays at 1:00 PM with Kinyorda. Building strength, flexibility and balance is one of the best ways to prevent falls.

## Neighborhood Advisor
Free and confidential for adults 60+. Our Neighborhood Advisor helps older adults get the services they need to stay independent at home, at your home or at the center. They can help you:

- Fill out medical applications and other forms.
- Find health care, legal advice, transportation, housing, nutrition, exercise, social activities and caregiver information.
- Learn about income-stretching programs such as SSI, SNAP, EPIC, HEAP and the IT-214 property tax credit.

Call Paige at 315.400.2844 or email paigem@westcottcc.org.

## Senior Companions
Volunteers who give friendship and help with daily tasks like shopping or paying bills, so adults can stay independent at home. There is no cost. Offered with InterFaith Works. Contact Paige at 315.400.2844.

## Transportation (United We Ride)
Free rides for adults 60+, and adults 18+ with diagnosed disabilities, Monday to Thursday, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. See [Senior Transportation](/programs/senior-transportation/).

## Disposable Briefs Bank
Free disposable briefs for older adults, given out twice a month at the center or delivered by DoorDash. Call Paige at 315.400.2844.

## Senior Technology and Senior Planet
iPad classes, computer and phone help on request, plus regular Senior Planet classes and workshops. Call Elise at 315.400.0982.

## Food pantry
Older adults in our Senior Services program can use the [Basic Needs Pantry](/programs/basic-needs-pantry/).

Greater Syracuse Works is a workforce development initiative that advocates for people across Central New York and works together to remove barriers to employment.

We connect employees, employers and service providers, with the goal of giving people the in-demand skills and credentials they need to succeed at work.

Our monthly direct service meetings give network members a place to review the state of workforce development, spot new opportunities, and build partnerships that create and sustain jobs for underserved people in the CNY region.

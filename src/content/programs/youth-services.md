Westcott Community Center runs quality after-school programs for youth in the Syracuse City School District. Serving more than 1,400 young people each year, we are committed to student success.

We work at 7 schools: **Webster Elementary, Ed Smith Pre-K-8, Clary Middle, Huntington Pre-K-8, Delaware, Dr. Weeks and Frazer**. We work closely with the district so that students take part in meaningful enrichment that builds on what they learn in class.

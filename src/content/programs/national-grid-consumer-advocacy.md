A National Grid Consumer Advocate is at the center on the **2nd Wednesday of every month**. This is a walk-in service, so appointments are not available.

Consumer Advocates help low-income and special-needs customers facing financial hardship. They offer early intervention, information on assistance programs, crisis bill-pay management, outreach and education. They also serve homebound customers and give talks on saving energy, safety, payment options and preparing for power outages.

Programs and payment options include:

- **Budget Plan.** Spreads payments evenly across the year. It is free and you can cancel any time. Call 800.433.1837 to enroll.
- **Deferred Payment Agreement.** If you have fallen behind, you may qualify to pay the past-due balance over time.
- **Bill Extender Plan.** If your main income is Social Security, a pension or disability benefits, you may get up to 10 extra days to pay without a late fee.
- **Consumer Appeals Team.** Talk through more payment options.

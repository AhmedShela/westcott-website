Westcott's Basic Needs Pantry serves the **13210, 13214 and 13224** zip codes, and any older adult who takes part in our Senior Services program.

- Households may visit the pantry **once a month**.
- The pantry is open **twice a month on Fridays**. Check the flyer for this season's dates.
- To be eligible, you self-attest to the Food Bank of CNY income guidelines and register with us when you arrive.

For more information, call 315.400.2225 or stop in.

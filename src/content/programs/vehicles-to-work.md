Westcott's Vehicles to Work coordinators help people enrolled in skilled training programs connected to the Syracuse Build initiative get past transportation barriers, including:

- restoring a suspended driver's license, or getting a license for the first time
- car repairs
- insurance problems
- shopping for a vehicle and getting financing

We also run group vehicle-literacy workshops. Our coordinators are certified credit counselors who teach every participant financial skills for the long term.

Vehicles to Work is an initiative of CenterState CEO.

Food $en$e is a monthly food-buying co-op for anyone who wants to stretch their grocery dollars. Each month there is a box of 12 to 15 staple grocery items at a discounted price.

Each package costs **$20.50**, and you can buy as many as you like. The items change from month to month, but every package includes:

- 4 to 5 meat items, like chicken, ground beef or fish
- 4 to 5 pantry staples, like pasta, soup and rice
- 2 fresh produce items, like apples, carrots or oranges

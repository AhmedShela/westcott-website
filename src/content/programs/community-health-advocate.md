Our Community Health Advocacy Program uses education, advocacy, nutrition and case management to take a whole-person approach to good health.

We also work with qualifying Medicaid members on health-related social needs such as food, housing and transportation, and connect them to the services they are eligible for.

Call our Community Health Advocate, Danielle Peterson, at 315.400.2988 to find out if this program is right for you.

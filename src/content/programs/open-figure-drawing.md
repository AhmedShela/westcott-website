Open Figure Drawing is a community drawing group that gives people of all abilities the chance to draw from an unclothed model. Westcott Community Center proudly supports these classes, which now meet at Delavan Studios.

The group aims to:

- offer an inexpensive drawing experience to the whole community
- give artists and students access to a live model
- offer drop-in drawing with no commitment
- keep a reliable, long-term schedule without breaks
- build a supportive artist community for sharing shows, openings, workshops and grants
- organize group shows of participants' work
- support and train artist models

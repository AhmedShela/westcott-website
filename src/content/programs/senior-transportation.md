Westcott Community Center offers free senior transportation through the Onondaga County Department of Adult and Long-Term Care Services' **United We Ride** program.

Rides are free for adults 60+ living on Syracuse's east side. Adults 18+ with diagnosed disabilities can also ride. We do not have a wheelchair-accessible vehicle, but we can take most walking aids. Passengers must be able to step in and out of the van with minor help.

We can give regular or one-time rides to our daily [lunch program](/programs/senior-services/), doctor appointments, grocery shopping, banking and more.

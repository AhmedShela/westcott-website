Our High School Equivalency classes help community members build their basic academic skills through focused courses in the core subjects. The program meets you where you are in your education goals and focuses on the skills you need to pass the GED exam and earn your high school equivalency diploma. Extra tutoring is available to help you succeed.

For more information, call SCSD Adult Education at 315.435.4115, or just stop by when class is in session.
